# IKA AI

**Version:** 0.1

## Vision

IKA AI is an AI-powered market analysis platform designed to help identify high-quality trading opportunities through data analysis, technical indicators, AI reasoning, and disciplined risk management.

The goal is to assist decision making first, then—only after extensive testing—optionally support automated trading.

---

## Objectives

- Analyse stocks and gold
- Provide AI-generated trade ideas
- Learn from historical performance
- Support paper trading
- Include strict risk management
- Desktop application with a mobile companion
